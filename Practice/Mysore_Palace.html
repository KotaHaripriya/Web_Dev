Mysore Palace

Mysore Palace, also known as Amba Vilas Palace, is one of India's most magnificent royal palaces.
Built between 1897 and 1912 in the Indo-Saracenic style, it served as the residence of the Wadiyar dynasty.
The palace is famous for its grand halls, intricate carvings, stained-glass windows, and beautiful gardens.
It is especially stunning during the Dasara festival, when it is illuminated with thousands of lights,
attracting visitors from around the world.
