About Ladakh

Ladakh is a beautiful region in northern India, known as the "Land of High Passes." It is famous for its snow-covered mountains, clear lakes, and Buddhist monasteries. The region attracts tourists with places like Pangong Lake and its breathtaking scenery. Ladakh is a perfect destination for adventure, nature lovers, and those seeking peace.
